## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Project scaffolded with rakvor-tool-forge (rust profile) on 2026-11-11.
- `analyze` command (`scan` kept as a hidden alias): audio and video authenticity signals for MP4/MOV/M4A/3GP,
  WAV (RIFF/RF64), Ogg (Opus/Vorbis) and MP3, with 37 media findings (structure, encoder traces, times,
  durations, metadata), each worded in EN/ES/FR with what it shows and what it does not prove.
- `rv-avauth-media` crate: bounded pure-Rust parsers (`#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`), encoder classification,
  signals and the structural reference fingerprint; hostile-input and byte-mutation tests.
- Worker-process isolation per media file (`--worker-timeout`, `--worker-memory`), second-read re-hash
  (`EVIDENCE_CHANGED_DURING_ANALYSIS`), `PARSER_FAILED` on crash, hang or memory limit.
- `--reference FILE`: comparison of 11 structural aspects with a file from the claimed device or app.
- Per-item `structure.json`, `metadata.json`, `reference-comparison.json`, hashed and audited; media section in
  the technical HTML/PDF/JSON reports.
- GUI: section 5 (reference file, time limit per file, audited `ui_reference_changed`,
  `ui_worker_timeout_changed`) and a per-file result list; headless screenshot example.
- Synthetic samples generated by `tests/make_samples.py` with `expected_findings.json` as ground truth.
- ADR 0002: v0.1 scope and assumptions.
